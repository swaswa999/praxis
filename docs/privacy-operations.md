# Mayter website privacy operations

Updated September 25, 2026. This procedure supports the website-only Privacy policy. It does not authorize new collection, outbound campaigns, or robotics-data programs.

## Current scope

- The public contact supplied by the owner is swayam@praxis.com. Use it for correspondence and requests until a replacement is confirmed.
- Signup stores email, timestamps, source, and notice version in Cloudflare D1. No automatic email sender is configured in this application.
- The Mayter and legacy TorqueSpec deployments use the same database; a request applies to that shared signup record.
- No incorporated entity, jurisdiction, postal address, or jurisdiction-specific compliance certification is represented on the site. Add confirmed operator details when supplied.

## Manual requests

1. Monitor the public inbox. For an opt-out, stop planned outreach to the address before any other processing.
2. Verify proportionately before disclosing or changing personal information. Do not ask for identity documents routinely.
3. Identify the exact email record in the intended Cloudflare account and D1 database. Preview the matching record before any mutation; do not export other subscribers.
4. For unsubscribe/removal, remove the address from the active signup database and any separate sending lists. Preserve only the minimum opt-out record needed to prevent reimporting it. Use authenticated operator tools; never add a public endpoint that reveals subscriber membership.
5. Confirm completion to the requester. Apply any deadlines required by the laws that apply to the operator and requester.
6. Retain signup records while the update list is active and the person remains subscribed. Remove active records if the list closes. Handle backups and hosting logs according to provider settings and any applicable legal need.

## Before sending development emails

The website signup records a request; it does not verify ownership or send an email. Confirm the operator identity, audience, sending provider, sender details, applicable consent requirements, postal address if required, and an opt-out mechanism before starting a campaign. Preserve historical notice versions; a duplicate signup does not imply consent to an unrelated new purpose.

## Change control

Update public notices before introducing new information categories, tracking, providers with different purposes, or training-data collection. A repair-recording program needs its own permission process. Do not treat the website email signup as permission to train robotics models.
